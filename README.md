Task 12 - Tableau Data Visualization

Dataset: E-commerce Sales Dataset
Tool: Tableau Public

Objective

To create different visualizations using the E-commerce Sales Dataset and analyze product categories, sales trends, regional profitability, quantity, and overall business performance.

Visualizations Created:
Sales by Category - Bar Chart
Sales Trend Over Time - Line Chart
Profit by Region - Bar Chart
Quantity vs Sales - Scatter Plot
Interactive Filters:
Category Filter
Region Filter
Interactive Dashboard:

All four visualizations are combined into a single interactive E-commerce Sales Dashboard. The Category and Region filters allow users to interactively analyze specific categories and regions.

Business Questions & Answers:
1. Which product categories generate higher sales?

→ The Sales by Category Bar Chart compares sales across different product categories and identifies the categories contributing more to total sales.

2. How do sales change over time?

→ The Sales Trend Line Chart shows the changes in sales over different dates and helps identify increasing or decreasing sales trends.

3. Which regions generate higher profit?

→ The Profit by Region Bar Chart compares regional profit and identifies the most and least profitable regions.

4. Is there a relationship between quantity and sales?

→ The Quantity vs Sales Scatter Plot shows the relationship between the number of products sold and the sales generated.

Business Insights:
Sales performance varies across different product categories.
Sales change over time, showing periods of higher and lower business activity.
Some regions contribute more profit than other regions.
Higher product quantities can contribute to higher sales, although the relationship may differ across products.
The interactive filters make it easier to compare specific categories and regions.
Recommendations:
Focus marketing and inventory efforts on high-performing product categories.
Improve strategies for low-profit regions through targeted promotions and better product availability.
Conclusion:

The Bar Chart, Line Chart, and Scatter Plot provide a clear visual understanding of the E-commerce Sales Dataset. These visualizations help analyze category sales, sales trends, regional profitability, and the relationship between quantity and sales. The interactive dashboard and filters support better business analysis and decision-making.
